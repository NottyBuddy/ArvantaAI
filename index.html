Call Scripts & Objections

Approved call flow, golden rules, discovery, renovation handling and ACE objection replies.

Golden Rules
  • Treat every call as a helpful consultation, not a rigid questionnaire.
  • Ask one question at a time; listen actively and acknowledge the customer.
  • Never criticise competitors or local fabricators.
  • Never promise unauthorised discounts, exact delivery/installation dates, technical specifications, measurements or design feasibility.
  • Use “Experience Centre”, “free site consultation” and “warranty”. Avoid “showroom”, “guarantee” and “replace”.
  • Every connected call must end with an agreed next action; reconfirm requirement, team, date and time.
Opening & Brand Introduction

“Good morning, Sir/Ma’am. I’m [Agent Name] calling from IndiFrame. We specialise in uPVC and aluminium windows and doors. We received your enquiry through [Source]. Is this a good time for a quick conversation?”

If the customer is unfamiliar: “IndiFrame provides windows and doors in different designs and configurations. Our team will understand your requirements and guide you on suitable options.”

Six Mandatory Discovery Questions
  1. Is this for a residential home or commercial property?
  2. Which city, area/locality and PIN code is the site in?
  3. Are you managing the build, or is an architect/interior designer/contractor involved?
  4. What stage is the site at — foundation, brickwork, plastering or finishing?
  5. Approximately how many window and door openings are required?
  6. Are internal and external plastering completed around all openings?

Ask conversationally, one question at a time. Verify serviceability and readiness before assigning the stage.

Mandatory Renovation Rule

Confirm whether the customer will arrange removal of existing windows and doors. IndiFrame does not provide uninstalling services. Work can begin only after the existing units are completely removed.

Ask when removal/site readiness is expected, then confirm a convenient follow-up date and time. Never confirm installation before removal is complete.

ACE — Local Fabricator Is Cheaper

Acknowledge: “I understand. Budget is an important part of any construction or renovation.”

Clarify: “When comparing quotations, have you checked whether the written warranty, hardware, glass, installation and after-sales support are included?”

Establish next step: “Our team can review the quotation line by line with you, with no obligation. Would a free site consultation or a sales callback be more convenient?”

Do not disparage the local fabricator or make unsupported claims about their materials.

Other Common Objections

Only exploring: “That’s completely fine. Planning early can help avoid rushed decisions. May I understand a couple of basic details so we can guide you when you’re ready?”

Send details on WhatsApp: “Of course. Is this for a full house or a few rooms? I’ll share the relevant details and arrange a follow-up after you’ve reviewed them.”

Price / discount: “Our range starts from ₹450 per sq ft onwards plus applicable GST. Exact pricing depends on sizes, types and quantity; our team can provide a quotation after consultation.”

Project postponed: “No problem. Roughly when do you expect the site to be ready? I’ll note a suitable follow-up.”

Closing & Handover

Reconfirm customer name, quantity/type of windows and doors, project location, agreed next action, responsible team, date and time. Update CRM on the same day. C2 leads: share details in the designated Daily Leads WhatsApp group and tag the regional sales owner.

FAQ Bank

Approved answers for company, product, pricing, delivery, warranty and process.

What is IndiFrame?

IndiFrame is a windows and doors brand headquartered in Hyderabad. It supports customers through consultation, design guidance, manufacturing, delivery, installation and after-sales service.

How are IndiFrame and Green Fortune related?

They are two brands under the same company. Green Fortune is the uPVC profile/material brand; IndiFrame supplies and installs finished windows and doors. Do not describe this as simply a company name change.

What is uPVC?

uPVC means unplasticised polyvinyl chloride. It is a rigid, durable, low-maintenance material used for windows and doors. Depending on the overall configuration, it can provide insulation and may help reduce outside noise and heat.

Which products and styles are available?

uPVC windows and doors are offered in styles including sliding, casement, fixed, tilt & turn, slide & fold and other configurations. Aluminium enquiries must be routed to Sales; agents must not quote aluminium prices, specifications or timelines.

What is the starting price?

“Starts from ₹450 per sq ft onwards, plus 18% GST.” This baseline covers standard white colour, clear glass and 60 series. Installation and after-sales are included; transport is extra. Do not calculate custom totals, per-window rates, premiums or discounts.

What is the delivery timeline?

Three to four weeks is the minimum delivery time from order confirmation. Never promise a delivery or installation date. Actual timing depends on order size, product mix, glass/colour selection and production load; Sales/Operations confirms the specific timeline.

What is the warranty?

Profile: 15 years. Hardware: 5 years operational warranty. Colour: 7 years on solid grey/solid black, 15 years on solid white and 20 years on laminated finishes. For exclusions or claim interpretation, hand over to the appropriate Sales/Service team.

Is site consultation chargeable?

No. The initial site consultation is free and carries no obligation.

Does IndiFrame install the windows?

Yes. The end-to-end process includes consultation, measurements, manufacturing, delivery, installation by the trained team and after-sales support.

What if a customer reports an issue after installation?

Capture the issue, affected window/room, when it started and the site address. Log and route it to the service team. Do not diagnose it or decide warranty coverage on the call.

Brand & Product

Approved brand story, uPVC in 90 seconds, systems, colours and proof points.

Who We Are

IndiFrame is a pan-India windows and doors brand headquartered in Hyderabad. It manages consultation, design, manufacturing, delivery, installation and after-sales service through one company.

Green Fortune supplies uPVC profiles/material to fabricators; IndiFrame builds, delivers and installs the finished window or door. Same company, two brands and distinct customer propositions.

uPVC in 90 Seconds

uPVC means unplasticised polyvinyl chloride. It is a rigid engineered material, not ordinary flexible PVC. It does not rust or rot and generally needs no painting. Its hollow chambers help with insulation; galvanised steel reinforcement contributes to frame strength. Keep explanations simple and defer technical ratings/specifications to the technical team.

Window Systems

Sliding, casement, fixed, tilt & turn, slide & fold, bay & bow, combination, top hung, vertical slider, mono rail, glass-to-glass, arch and villa window. Agents identify the type only; Sales/Design confirms suitability and feasibility.

Door Systems

Sliding, casement and lift & slide options are referenced in the approved product material. Route configuration-specific questions to Sales.

Colours & Warranty

Approved finish range includes white, solid grey, solid black and laminated finishes such as red oak, dark oak, walnut, grey laminate and black laminate. Warranty: 7 years on solid grey/black, 15 years on solid white and 20 years on laminated finishes. Confirm any colour-specific availability with Sales.

Eight Proof Points
  • One company manages the end-to-end job.
  • In-house product/profile design and technical selection.
  • One-acre Medchal factory with European and Turkish CNC machinery.
  • 50+ trained technicians; capacity up to 80,000 sq ft/month.
  • More than 5,600 projects and 3.31 million sq ft executed.
  • Own trained installation team.
  • Written/digital warranty and structured service process.
  • Typical delivery lead time is 3–4 weeks minimum from order confirmation.

Use only two or three proof points relevant to the customer's concern.
