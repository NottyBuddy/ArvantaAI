Call Scripts & Objections

Both approved scripts are available below. Use the version that matches the call scenario and follow current operating controls.

1. Final Call Script — Scenario-wise Full Script

Golden rules: Treat every call as a helpful consultation; ask one question at a time; listen actively; explain simply; do not criticise competitors; do not promise unauthorised discounts, exact delivery dates, technical specifications, structural approvals or design feasibility; every connected call ends with a clear next action.

Opening & discovery

“Good morning, Sir/Ma’am. I’m [Agent Name], calling from IndiFrame Windows & Doors regarding your enquiry. Is this a good time for a quick conversation?”

“May I quickly understand your project so I can guide you to the right team?”

  1. Is this for a residential home, villa, apartment or commercial property?
  2. Which city, locality and PIN code is the site in?
  3. Are you managing the project, or is an architect, contractor or designer involved?
  4. What stage is the site at — foundation, slab, brickwork, plastering or finishing?
  5. Approximately how many window and door openings do you need?
  6. Is internal and external plastering complete around all openings?

Brand / uPVC explanation

“IndiFrame provides uPVC windows and doors in different designs and configurations. uPVC means Unplasticised Polyvinyl Chloride. It is a rigid, durable and low-maintenance material. Depending on the complete configuration, it can help with insulation and reduce outside noise and heat.”

Renovation — mandatory sequence

  1. Ask whether the customer/their team will remove existing windows and doors.
  2. Explain that IndiFrame does not provide uninstalling services; existing units must be fully removed before work starts.
  3. Confirm customer acceptance.
  4. If accepted, capture expected removal/site-readiness date and follow-up date/time.
  5. Never confirm installation before existing units are removed.

Suggested line: “Since this is a renovation, the existing windows and doors will need to be removed before our work starts. Will you be able to arrange their removal?”

Scenario A — Digital / fresh enquiry

Confirm enquiry source, permission to speak, product requirement, project type, location, stage, quantity and readiness. Explain the next step and schedule a callback/site consultation/Experience Centre visit as applicable.

Scenario B — Referral / direct / walk-in enquiry

Acknowledge the referral or visit, confirm who referred the customer, reconfirm windows/doors required and complete the six discovery questions before routing.

Scenario C — Expo / event lead

“Thank you for visiting our stall and showing interest in IndiFrame. The best next step is a free site consultation with our sales team. They can understand your project and guide you on suitable options.”

Scenario D — Warm lead / stage follow-up

“We spoke earlier regarding your window and door requirements for [Project/Location]. I wanted to check how the project is progressing. Have there been any updates?” Reconfirm quantity/type, quotation review, construction stage and next action. Recommend installation planning after granite/marble sill work is complete.

Scenario E — Outside direct service area / partner

Explain that direct installation coverage is selective and offer connection to an authorised Green Fortune partner. Capture full project details and record C7 / ROUTE TO B2F. Do not promise full IndiFrame service for partner-routed leads.

Objection replies

Local fabricator cheaper: “I understand. When comparing quotations, it helps to compare the complete scope — consultation, measurements, manufacturing, installation, warranty and after-sales support. Our team can help you review the details.”

Only exploring: “That’s absolutely fine. May I understand a couple of basic details so we can guide you whenever you’re ready?”

Just send details: “Certainly. Are you looking for windows, doors or both? I’ll share the relevant details and note a suitable follow-up.”

Only want price: “Our uPVC range starts from ₹450 per sq ft onwards plus applicable GST. Final pricing depends on measurements, styles and quantity. May I confirm the approximate requirement?”

Busy: “I respect your time. When would be a convenient time for our team to call back?”

Remove my number: Apologise, record the request and follow the approved suppression process. Do not continue sales probing.

Already purchased / chose another brand: Acknowledge the decision, optionally ask a brief feedback question, and close courteously.

Golden closing & checklist

Reconfirm customer name, quantity/type of windows and doors, project location, agreed next action, responsible team, date and time. Never end with only “our team will contact you”.

CRM fields: customer name, phone, lead source/sub-source, city, locality, address/landmark, project type, new construction/renovation, stage, window/door requirement, quantity, material preference, configuration, primary requirement, budget sensitivity, timeline, decision-maker, architect/contractor, competitor status, site consultation, Experience Centre interest, quotation status, objection, next action, follow-up date and disposition.

2. Updated Call Script — Current Operating Version

Call approach: Helpful consultation, not rigid questionnaire. Ask one question at a time. Listen, acknowledge, keep explanations simple, do not criticise competitors, do not promise unauthorised discounts or unverified technical/delivery details. Reconfirm requirement, next action, team, date and time.

Six mandatory discovery questions

  1. Project type — residential or commercial?
  2. Site location — city, area and PIN code?
  3. Stakeholder — self, architect, civil contractor or designer?
  4. Construction stage — foundation, brickwork, plastering or finishing?
  5. Approximate number of window and door openings?
  6. Internal and external plastering completed around all openings?

Lead stage & routing

  • C0 — New unqualified: protected-account check, then first call immediately.
  • C1 — Future potential: foundation/slab/brickwork/plastering incomplete; schedule CRM follow-up on projected readiness.
  • C2 — Active / qualified: plastering complete and openings ready; share in Daily Leads group, tag regional owner and offer free site consultation or Experience Centre visit.
  • C7 — Partner route: outside direct coverage; offer authorised Green Fortune partner and log C7 / ROUTE TO B2F.

Pricing & warranty guardrails

Quote only “starts from ₹450 per sq ft onwards plus applicable GST” for approved standard baseline. Do not calculate custom totals, single-window estimates or discounts. Route aluminium enquiries to Sales without quoting prices/specifications/timelines. Profile warranty 15 years; hardware operational warranty 5 years; colour warranty depends on finish as in approved warranty matrix.

Renovation rule

Customer/their contractor must remove existing windows and doors before IndiFrame work begins. IndiFrame does not provide uninstalling services. Confirm acceptance and site-ready/follow-up date; do not confirm installation before removal.

Unresponsive / difficult call handling

For blank calls or no response, use the approved polite opening twice and close if there is still no response. For abusive language, request professional language; if it continues, close courteously. For extended unattended hold, disconnect according to the approved call protocol. Log the exact disposition.

Follow-up scenarios

  • Requirement recap: reconfirm quantity/type and location.
  • Site visit pending: coordinate a convenient date/time with the technical team.
  • Site visit completed: ask whether the customer needs clarification or next-step discussion.
  • Quotation shared: ask whether they reviewed it and what needs clarification.
  • Price comparison: discuss scope, product suitability, hardware, installation, warranty and service; do not disparage competitor.
  • Family decision pending: agree a follow-up date/time.
  • Project delayed: record pause and agree when to check back.
  • Ready to proceed: route order confirmation and manufacturing next steps to the concerned team.

Factory / Experience Centre request

Log factory visit requests and tag the regional owner. Agents must not independently confirm a factory visit. Sales confirms the visit. Experience Centre locations are listed in Quick Reference.

Current retry policy

Maximum 15 attempts across 9 calendar days. This current approved rule supersedes older 9-attempt / 12-day wording found in legacy script sections.

Who is IndiFrame?

IndiFrame is a windows and doors brand handling consultation, manufacturing, installation and after-sales support through its own operating setup. It is headquartered in Hyderabad and serves approved markets in Maharashtra, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Where are you based? Where is the factory?

Head office and manufacturing are in Hyderabad. The one-acre manufacturing facility is at Medchal, Hyderabad, with advanced European and Turkish machinery.

Weren't you Green Fortune?

Green Fortune and IndiFrame are brands under the same company with distinct roles. Green Fortune is the uPVC profile/material brand and partner/fabricator ecosystem; IndiFrame is the finished windows and doors brand with manufacturing and installation.

Can I visit the factory?

“I’ll connect with our Sales team. They’ll confirm the details and guide you on the next steps.” Agents must log and tag the request; Sales confirms factory visits.

Do you have an Experience Centre?

Yes. Hyderabad Kukatpally and Mumbai Malad West are operating Experience Centres. Jubilee Hills flagship is listed as opening soon in the source; confirm current visit status with Sales before promising.

Is uPVC strong? Is it ordinary plastic?

uPVC means unplasticised polyvinyl chloride. It is a rigid engineered material, different from ordinary soft PVC, designed to retain its shape and provide durable, low-maintenance window and door frames.

Will uPVC yellow, crack or warp in heat?

The approved product material describes uPVC as engineered to resist heat-related warping and discolouration. Avoid absolute guarantees; route specific performance or technical-rating questions to Sales/Technical.

What colours are available?

What is DGU?

DGU means Double Glazed Unit. It uses two glass panes separated by a sealed air gap. The overall glazing configuration can help reduce outside noise and heat. Do not promise a specific acoustic or thermal rating.

Do you provide aluminium windows?

Route aluminium enquiries to Sales for current availability, pricing, specifications and timelines. Agents must not quote aluminium details independently.

Do you make doors?

Yes, IndiFrame offers uPVC doors. Door configurations include sliding, casement, lift & slide, French, slide & fold and combinations, subject to Sales/Design confirmation.

Do you provide railings or other door categories?

What is the starting price?

Approved baseline: starts from ₹450 per sq ft onwards plus 18% GST. This applies to the standard baseline specification; custom price depends on configuration, dimensions and quantity. Never calculate custom totals or per-window estimates.

What is included in the price?

Standard baseline includes custom windows, trained in-house installation and after-sales support. GST and transport are billed separately. Confirm the specific quotation inclusions with Sales.

“I’ll connect with our Sales team. They’ll confirm the technical and commercial details.” Do not quote discounts or unapproved hardware grades.

Yes. Initial site consultation is free and carries no obligation.

IndiFrame supports the end-to-end journey: consultation, professional measurements, custom manufacturing, delivery, trained installation and after-sales support.

1. Who We Are — Company in 60 Seconds

IndiFrame is a windows and doors brand of Traum build Adeshina Solutions Pvt. Ltd., headquartered in Hyderabad. It manufactures and installs uPVC windows and doors, with presence across Maharashtra, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

It manages the end-to-end journey: site consultation, design, manufacturing, delivery, installation and after-sales service. The one-acre Medchal campus has European and Turkish CNC machinery, capacity up to 80,000 sq ft/month and 50+ trained technicians. More than 3.31 million sq ft and 5,600+ projects are referenced in the approved SOP.

Core positioning: IndiFrame designs, manufactures and installs windows and doors end to end, from one company, with a written warranty.

Tagline in source SOP: “Where the World Meets Your Home.”

2. IndiFrame & Green Fortune — Two Brands, One Company

IndiFrame sells finished windows and doors to homeowners, builders, architects and designers. Green Fortune supplies uPVC profiles/raw material to fabricators and dealers. Same company, two brands, distinct customer propositions.

Approved response: “Green Fortune is our profile brand. As our finished windows business grew, it got its own identity as IndiFrame. Green Fortune supplies the material; IndiFrame builds, delivers and installs the complete window.” Do not say simply “we changed our company name”.

3. What We Actually Sell — End-to-End Accountability

Sell the finished job and accountability, not only the profile. IndiFrame coordinates site measurement, own-factory manufacturing, own trained installation team and written warranty. The differentiator is a coordinated service and support process.

Approved customer line: “We measure your site, manufacture at our own facility in Hyderabad, our own team installs it, and you get a written warranty from a registered company.”

4. Eight Proof Points
  1. One company handles design, measurement, manufacturing, delivery, installation and service.
  2. Own profile and tooling design; technical team vets components.
  3. One-acre Medchal factory, 50+ technicians, up to 80,000 sq ft/month.
  4. More than 5,600 projects and 3.31 million sq ft executed.
  5. Own trained installation team and in-house training school.
  6. Digital/written warranty: 15-year profile and 5-year operational hardware warranty.
  7. Formal service-request process and tracked after-sales support.
  8. Minimum typical delivery lead time 3–4 weeks from order confirmation.

Use only two or three proof points that answer the customer's actual concern. Never promise an exact delivery or installation date.

5. uPVC in 90 Seconds

uPVC means unplasticised polyvinyl chloride. It is rigid engineered plastic, unlike ordinary soft PVC. The profile is hollow by design; sealed chambers support insulation, while galvanised steel reinforcement contributes to frame strength. uPVC does not rust or rot and generally does not require painting.

Customer-friendly line: “It’s not ordinary soft plastic, Sir. It’s a rigid engineered frame, with galvanised steel reinforcement inside. The uPVC profile helps with insulation and a properly designed and installed window supports comfort against heat, noise, dust and rain.”

Keep performance claims conditional on the complete window configuration. Do not quote chamber counts, wall thickness, wind load, thermal ratings or sound ratings; route those to Technical/Sales.

6. Window Systems — Recognition Guide
  • Sliding: shutters move sideways on tracks.
  • Casement: hinged opening, inward or outward.
  • Fixed: non-opening glass panel for light and view.
  • Tilt & Turn: tilts for ventilation or opens fully.
  • Slide & Fold: panels fold away to open a wide span.
  • Bay & Bow: multi-section window projects outward.
  • Combination: two or more window types in one opening.
  • Top Hung: opens from the top.
  • Vertical Slider: opens vertically.
  • Mono Rail: sliding arrangement with one main track.
  • Glass-to-Glass: open glass appearance.
  • Arch / Villa: architectural or larger custom-style windows.

System names are for recognition only. Sales/Design confirms suitability and feasibility for the actual opening.

7. Door Systems
  • Sliding: moves on a track.
  • Casement: hinged door.
  • Lift & Slide: heavy-duty sliding mechanism for larger openings.
  • French: paired hinged doors opening from the centre.
  • Slide & Fold: panels fold away to open a wide span.
  • Combination: door with fixed panels as one assembly.
8. Recognise System Families

60 Series Casement; Monorail (Fixed + Sliding); 62 / 90 / 112 Series Sliding; 80 / 99 Series Sliding; Slide & Fold (2, 3 and 4 panel options); Tilt & Turn. Recognise the names only. Never quote sizes, heights, wall thickness, glazing figures or performance ratings.

9. Glass, Colours & Hardware

Glass: DGU means Double Glazed Unit — two panes separated by a sealed air gap. Confirm glass build-up and technical performance with Sales/Technical.

Finishes: White, Solid Grey, Solid Black, Red Oak, Dark Oak, Walnut, Grey Laminate and Black Laminate. Confirm live colour availability before committing.

Warranty: Profile 15 years; hardware 5-year operational warranty; solid grey/black colour 7 years; solid white 15 years; laminated finishes 20 years.

Features: mosquito mesh, grills and child locks are listed in the FAQ bank; confirm configuration and availability.

10. Site Readiness & Installation

For C2 qualification, plastering should be complete and apertures ready for audit. Installation is best planned after granite or marble sill work is complete. In renovation projects, customer/their team must remove existing windows and doors; IndiFrame does not provide uninstalling services. Never confirm installation before removal and site readiness.

11. Pricing — Approved Baseline Only

uPVC baseline starts from ₹450 per sq ft onwards plus 18% GST, for the approved standard baseline specification. Installation and after-sales support are included; transport is extra. Final pricing depends on dimensions, configuration, glass/colour selection and quantity.

Never calculate a custom total, per-window estimate, premium, discount or final quote. Route commercial questions to Sales.

12. Red Lines — What Agents Must Not Promise
  • No unauthorised discount, custom total or final price.
  • No exact delivery/installation date; 3–4 weeks is a minimum typical delivery time from order confirmation, not a date promise.
  • No mm-level specification, chamber count, wall thickness, wind-load, structural approval or technical performance rating.
  • No design feasibility or technical suitability confirmation.
  • No diagnosis or warranty eligibility decision for service complaints.
  • No negative statements about competitors or local fabricators.
  • No factory visit confirmation; log and tag the regional owner for Sales confirmation.
  • No promise of products not confirmed as launched or currently available.
13. Brand Voice — How IndiFrame Sounds
  • Confident about the outcome; calm about the method.
  • Advise, do not push. Let the customer finish speaking.
  • Be specific about agreed next steps, not vague.
  • Never oversell or promise what cannot be checked.
  • Speak the customer's preferred language — Telugu, Hindi or English — and switch naturally.
  • When unsure: “That’s a good question. Let me have our team give you the exact answer.”
